IMDB Sentiment Analysis — Model Comparison

Assignment 1 (Baseline):
- MLP (VADER + TextBlob features): 70.82%

Assignment 2 Models:

1. MLP + TF-IDF:
- Accuracy: 86.64%

2. 1D CNN:
- Accuracy: 87.89%

3. Hybrid CNN:
- Accuracy: 86.96%

4. LSTM:
- Accuracy: 69.12%


Analysis:

The Assignment 2 models significantly improved over the Assignment 1 baseline.

The 1D CNN achieved the best performance (87.89%), slightly outperforming both the TF-IDF MLP and the Hybrid CNN. This suggests that convolutional models are effective at capturing local sentiment patterns such as key phrases.

The MLP with TF-IDF also performed very well (86.64%), demonstrating that feature-based approaches can remain highly competitive.

The Hybrid CNN achieved similar performance (86.96%), showing that adding additional layers and regularization can maintain strong results.

The LSTM model performed worse in this experiment (69.12%). This is likely due to limited hyperparameter tuning and training time. Recurrent models typically require more careful optimization and longer training to reach their full potential.

Conclusion:

Overall, the experiments show that both TF-IDF-based models and CNN-based architectures significantly outperform the baseline. Among the tested models, the 1D CNN provided the best trade-off between performance and complexity.
